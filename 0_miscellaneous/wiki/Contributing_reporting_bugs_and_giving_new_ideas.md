## Contributing reporting bugs and giving new ideas

We encourage you to inform us in our [**issue tracker**](http://issues.openmarkov.org). When
informing, please make sure that your issue has not been already reported and that your version of
OpenMarkov is the latest.

Note the issue tracker has multiple forms depending on what you want to specify: A) A bug, B) A
complete new feature, or C) An enhancement of an existing feature.

Thanks in advance for your collaboration.
